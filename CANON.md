# ctrl+love Canon

This file is the canonical reference for future ctrl+love work. Read it before changing homepage, Room, pricing, ambassador, persona, or institutional copy.

## Identity

ctrl+love is not an AI consultancy, SaaS company, productivity platform, or agency website.

ctrl+love is:
- an institution
- a decision laboratory
- a growing global network
- a philosophy made tangible

The homepage should feel like entering an institution, not browsing a product page.

## Founder

There is one Founder: Poppe van Pelt.

Never use founder language that implies multiple founders, co-founders, leadership, or a leadership team. The Founder section exists to explain why ctrl+love exists, not to present a resume.

Canonical founder note:

> Thirty years helping organisations make decisions taught me that most expensive mistakes were not caused by a lack of intelligence. They happened because nobody challenged certainty early enough. ctrl+love exists to change that.

## Human Ambassadors

Ambassadors are real people across real countries with real expertise. They are separate from Synthetic Personas.

Only include ambassadors who are officially accepted and marked public in `src/app/ambassadors-data.ts`. Do not invent ambassadors. Do not promote prospects.

Safiyyah must not appear until officially accepted.

## Flagship Products

The three canonical commercial products are:
- Decision Stress-Test™
- On-Call Room™
- Kill or Scale™

Do not rename them. Do not replace them with invented products.

Each product should explain:
- what it is
- who it is for
- what outcome it delivers

## Approved Case Study Pattern

Case studies are proof. Treat them as flagship content.

Each case should communicate:
- sector
- decision question
- blind spot
- Room outcome
- one memorable insight

Do not use generic examples when approved anonymised cases exist.

## Institutional Objects

The Steel Ball is canonical. Keep it as a symbolic object.

The Constitution is canonical. Keep it as institutional law and mythology.

Decision Artifacts are canonical. They are the written trace of what changed in The Room.

The Museum is canonical. It should feel like part of the institution, not a side joke.

## Homepage Narrative

Canonical Chapter 1 order:
1. Arrival: Shortcut to Reality.
2. Enter The Room: live decision.
3. What happened?
4. Meet the Synthetic Minds.
5. Products: Decision Stress-Test™, On-Call Room™, Kill or Scale™.
6. Selected Decisions: approved anonymised case studies.
7. The Steel Ball.
8. Global Ambassador Network.
9. Founder.
10. The Constitution.
11. Bring your decision into the Room.

Every chapter should answer one question. Do not mix purposes.

## Voice Principles

Preserve identity over novelty.

Preserve canon over optimization.

Preserve authorship over invention.

Choose institution over SaaS convention.

Choose mythology over minimalism when the choice matters.

Motion should reinforce behavior: confidence shifts, participants enter, objects react, and the Room changes its mind.

## Things That Must Never Change Without Explicit Approval

- Poppe van Pelt is the single Founder.
- Synthetic Personas and Human Ambassadors are separate.
- The three flagship products are Decision Stress-Test™, On-Call Room™, and Kill or Scale™.
- The Steel Ball remains a canonical object.
- The Constitution remains part of the mythology.
- The Room is the emotional center of the institution.
- ctrl+love must never be framed as a generic consultancy, SaaS product, agency, or AI wrapper.
