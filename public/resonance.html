RESEARCH PROTOCOL / FIRST PILOT

Observe first. Interpret afterwards.

Use a single performance, vary condition order, mark freely, annotate afterwards and compare timelines. This checklist is local to the page and does not collect participant data.

Research boundary. Clicks are subjective reports. Acoustic features are not proof of coordination. Simulations are not physiological measurements. Any future human-subject physiological research requires independent oversight and informed adult consent.
