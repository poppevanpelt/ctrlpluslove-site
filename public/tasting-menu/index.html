01 / Choose your ingredients

What are you hungry for?

This example follows a matcha business from the first visit to the next refill. Each instrument tackles one real question, returns something useful, and comes with a small experiment to test its value.

This is a proposal menu. The examples are illustrative; these instruments are not connected to any business systems or customer data.

02 / A little taste, right now

When does the tin run out?

A daily ritual has a surprisingly practical question: when do you need more matcha?

Try the Refill Clock. Change the inputs to see how different habits change the refill window. This is a simple consumption estimate, not a forecast of customer behaviour.

Formula: grams ÷ grams per cup ÷ cups per day. The buffer determines when a refill reminder could be useful. All values are editable assumptions.
