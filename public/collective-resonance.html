How to read the fingerprint

Each row is an invented observer. Orange marks are reported clicks. The lower curve counts distinct observers with at least one mark within the chosen tolerance of each time point. The dashed line is an invented expected cue, not a measured stimulus event. Wider tolerance can inflate apparent agreement.

Agreement is descriptive, not evidence of shared emotion or causation. Future studies require informed consent, independent review, preregistration, and uncertainty estimates.
