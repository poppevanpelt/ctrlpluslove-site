INSTRUMENT 008 · EXPERIMENT 03 · RESEARCH SANDBOX

Human resonance.

Explore how acoustic events, visible cues, hypothetical physiological responses and conscious reports might relate in time. A research design instrument, not a measurement of a person.

SIMULATED DATA ONLY. All traces and clicks are invented for demonstration. No sensors, camera, personal health data or predictive targeting. No inference of emotion or persuasion.

Future supervised adult studies: consent, baseline, sensor quality, withdrawal and independent review.

Randomized condition order, session timing and repeated exposure.

What would count as evidence?

Preregister a hypothesis, compare conditions, account for sensor lag and repeated observations, report uncertainty and null results. A physiological change does not establish enjoyment, attention, persuasion or a causal effect.

Commercial research should use aggregate, consent-based creative evaluation rather than individual biometric profiling or targeting.
