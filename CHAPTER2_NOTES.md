# Chapter 2 Notes

Created: 2026-07-14

## Observations From Chapter 1

The homepage now creates a canonical sample decision: Decision 014, Brazil expansion next quarter. This should either remain the canonical decision across Chapter 2 surfaces or be deliberately replaced with a richer sample before wider implementation.

The Decision Artifact content model used on the homepage is intentionally compact:

- Original Decision
- Pressure Applied
- Blind Spot
- Decision
- Confidence
- Reality Contact

This model can seed the more complete artifact system later, but it should not be treated as final for every route.

The homepage now uses institutional navigation language:

- The Room
- The Museum
- Artifacts
- The Constitution
- Admission

Other pages may still use older site language. Chapter 2 should reconcile this carefully rather than forcing every route into the new taxonomy at once.

## Dependencies To Resolve Later

The Room page still needs to become a live disagreement surface rather than a persona directory.

Living Decision Review should become the canonical proof page, ideally using the same decision arc or a closely related artifact system.

Pricing still needs to become Diagnosis around the consequence the visitor wants to avoid.

The Admission path needs a more resilient inquiry mechanism than mailto if this becomes a production conversion path.

The Constitution page should eventually show more visible articles and annotations, but Chapter 1 only required Article I on the homepage.

The Museum and Steel Ball should connect each object more directly to a Room behavior and decision lesson.

## Ideas Not Implemented

Animate confidence changes with timed pauses. Deferred because the brief asked for minimal, non-decorative motion and the first implementation should prove the sequence without relying on animation.

Create a fuller artifact archive entry for Decision 014. Deferred until the artifact system is defined across routes.

Introduce Poppe as founder-editor near the first Room. Deferred because the first experience is stronger when the Room, not the founder, earns trust.

Add a form-based Admission ritual. Deferred because it would touch conversion infrastructure and was outside Chapter 1's narrow homepage mandate.

Use a richer real-world decision with more evidence. Deferred until there is client-safe material or a chosen canonical sample.

## Chapter 2 Starting Point

Start with the Room page.

The homepage now promises that perspectives enter and change judgment. The next strongest move is to make `/room/` show that behavior in more detail, then let Living Decision Review become the deeper proof.
